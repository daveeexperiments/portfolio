Hi! I am David, aka Davee.

I'm a game developer. C++ and the Gameplay Ability System for Unreal Engine 5, GDScript for Godot, TypeScript for browser games, and plain paper for card games. The engine changes. The questions mostly don't.

Most of what I enjoy is the part underneath the game. Movement that feels right. Tools that let a designer work without opening code. The small systems nobody notices until they're missing.

Games!

My time splits between studio work and the things I build on my own bench.

At Dimension11 Studios I work on Legends of Orisha: Blood and Water, a third-person action-adventure rooted in Yoruba mythology, built in Unreal Engine 5 as part of ID@Xbox.

On my own, I'm building a slingshot ball platformer. You fling a ball up a vertical climb and swap its material mid-flight, squishy or rocky, which decides what you bounce off and what you smash through. I'm also making Mushrealm, a tabletop card game about Trees, Beasts and Mushrooms.

All of it gets filed in the lab, with the question each one is testing attached.
